How it works

From voice to structured notes

Three simple steps. Everything happens in your browser.

01

Speak

Press the microphone and talk naturally. Auralis listens using your browser’s built-in speech recognition.

02

Transcribe

Speech is converted to text in real time. Interim results appear as you speak; final results lock in the transcript.

03

Structure

A local note-extraction engine turns the transcript into headings, key ideas, terms, and takeaways — no cloud AI required.

Privacy

Designed around local processing

Browser speech recognition

Auralis uses the Web Speech API provided by your browser. Recognition may be processed by the browser vendor (e.g. Google on Chrome). We do not send audio to any custom server.

Local storage only

Transcripts and notes are stored in your browser’s localStorage. They never leave your device unless you explicitly copy or download them.

Microphone permission

Access is requested only when you press the microphone button. You can revoke permission at any time in your browser settings. The mic is stopped as soon as recording ends.

No accounts, no tracking

This MVP has no sign-up, no analytics, and no third-party tracking scripts. Everything runs client-side.
